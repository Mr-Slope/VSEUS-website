VSEUS is UBC's newest undergraduate society. That's a strange thing to still be able to
say, given how long the Vancouver School of Economics has existed without one, but here
we are: representing students at the #1 economics school in the country, and building
the infrastructure that should have existed years ago.

## What we do

Everything we run falls under one of four pillars.

- **Academic success**: supporting students in their coursework and creating real
  opportunities to connect with professors, formally and informally.
- **Student advocacy**: representing student interests to UBC, the Vancouver School of
  Economics, the Alma Mater Society, and anyone else whose decisions affect you.
- **Career preparedness**: connecting students with professionals in the industry and
  building actual workplace readiness, not just a resume template.
- **Community engagement**: raising school spirit and building an environment where
  Economics students feel like they belong somewhere specific, not just enrolled in a
  program.

We've written more about what each of these costs us in practice in
[What the Four Pillars Actually Mean](/blog/what-the-four-pillars-actually-mean).

## The numbers so far

- **1,000+** economics students represented
- **50+** events and initiatives a year
- **33%** increase in funding
- **15+** campus collaborations

None of these numbers are the point on their own. They're what happens when advocacy,
programming, and partnerships actually get built instead of just promised.

## Our vision

VSEUS exists to make sure every member continually engages with, and feels a genuine
sense of belonging and pride in, the Economics community and the wider UBC community.
Students should be exposed to the range of fields within Economics so they're informed
about their future paths and feel prepared for life after graduation. That means working
with students, faculty, and staff to improve accessibility at VSE and UBC, and
continually reassessing what students actually need rather than assuming we already
know.

## Our mission

We create and facilitate spaces where members can comfortably interact with each other,
share their stories, and build the relationships that make an Economics community out of
a shared course code. We maintain a clear feedback channel between the VSEUS Council and
our members, hold frequent consultations, and take transparency seriously rather than
treating it as a talking point.

We also look outward: building relationships with other groups who share our vision so
we can learn from what already works, and building opportunities internally for members
to volunteer within the organization itself. Long term, we want VSEUS to establish
traditions strong enough that students feel proud to say they were part of this
community, not just enrolled during it.

If any of this doesn't match what you're experiencing as a student in the program,
[tell us](/contact). The vision only means something if it holds up against what's
actually happening.
